01 / Motion-OS

Architecture & development

The work centres on simulation, state transitions and measurable behaviour. Technical prototypes exist; industrial deployment remains a validation objective.

Prototype work

Browser simulations

Interactive particle and field experiments developed in browser-based environments. Performance figures will be published with reproducible test conditions.

Architecture

Deterministic state logic

Research into explicit state transitions, motion-based interaction and causal inspection within bounded simulations.

Governance concept

Auditable behaviour

Proposed controls include bounded state retention and traceable system decisions, subject to independent validation.
